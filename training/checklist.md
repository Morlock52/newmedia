# Training Deliverables Checklist

- Objectives: 3–5 measurable outcomes for each module
- Agenda: durations, breaks, prerequisites, materials
- Slides: narrative flow, visuals, accessibility, timing cues
- Instructor Guide: step-by-step with talking points and demos
- Workbook: exercises, spaces for answers, reflection prompts
- Handouts: quick reference, commands, diagrams, cheatsheets
- Assessment: knowledge checks (per section) + final challenge
- Feedback: short learner survey link or QR code
- Logistics: room/virtual setup, recording plan, backup materials
- Post-course: follow-up resources, next steps, office hours

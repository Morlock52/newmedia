## Overview

This guide provides step-by-step facilitation notes aligned to the slides. Use the time boxes as pacing guards and adapt based on learner signals.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain the problem space and goals in plain language
- Navigate the environment and find key resources
- Complete a basic task end-to-end with guidance

## Materials

- Slides: `slides.html`
- Workbook: `workbook.md`
- Handout: `handouts/quick-reference.md`

## Agenda & Pacing

| Section | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome & Orientation | 5 min | Intake expectations; logistics |
| Concepts | 10 min | Use visuals; anchor to audience |
| Demo | 20 min | Narrate intent; pause strategically |
| Activity | 10 min | Pairs; circulate and assist |
| Q&A | 5 min | Capture parking-lot items |

## Slide-by-Slide Notes

### Welcome (Slides 1–2)

- Set tone; invite intros (name, role, goal)
- Share how questions will be handled (chat, verbal, parking lot)

### Concepts (Slides 3–4)

- Connect to learner context; reduce jargon
- Emphasize outcomes over features

### Demo (Slides 5–6)

- Script: “We’ll go from zero to a working example.”
- Call out pitfalls and error recovery paths

### Activity (Slide 7)

- Timer: 8 minutes focused work + 2 minute share-out
- Encourage pairs; celebrate “near misses” as learning opportunities

### Knowledge Check (Slide 8)

- Cold-call gently or use polls; keep it light

### Recap & Next (Slide 9)

- Summarize, preview Module 02, share support channels

## Risk & Mitigations

- Environment drift → Have a recorded demo and screenshots
- Varied skill levels → Offer stretch goals and safety rails
- Time overruns → Pre-decide cuts (examples > extra theory)
